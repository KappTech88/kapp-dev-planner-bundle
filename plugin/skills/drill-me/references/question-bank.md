# Question bank for /drill-me

Use these as follow-ups when an answer is vague. Ask one at a time.

## Pitch and problem
- Describe the moment a user reaches for this. What were they doing one minute earlier?
- What do they do today instead? What does that cost them (time, money, errors)?
- If this existed and worked, what would they stop doing?
- Is this a new capability or a replacement? What must it match to be adopted?

## Users
- Who is the first user (name a real person or role)? Who is the tenth?
- Who pays, who uses, who administers? Are they the same person?
- What is their technical level? What devices and environments?
- How many users in month one, month six?

## Success
- What number goes up or down? By how much, by when?
- What would make you call v1 a failure even if it "works"?
- What is the one thing it must never do?

## Scope
- What is the smallest version you would actually use daily?
- Which feature would you cut first if you lost half the time?
- What are you tempted to add that should wait?
- Which integrations are required on day one versus nice to have?

## Constraints
- Deadline or event driving the timeline? Fixed or flexible?
- Where does it run: local machine, server, cloud, mobile, browser? Offline needed?
- Data sensitivity: personal data, credentials, payments, health? Regulations?
- License constraints on dependencies or on the output?
- Existing code, databases, or APIs it must work with? Are they documented?

## Tech stack
- What have you or the team shipped with before? What did you enjoy or regret?
- Any hard requirement (language, framework, cloud, database) from the organization?
- Performance or scale needs that rule options in or out?
- Does it need a GUI, a TUI, a CLI, an API, or several?
- Will AI/LLM features be part of it? Local models or hosted APIs?

## Risks and unknowns
- What part are you least sure how to build?
- Which dependency or third party could block you?
- What would you want to prototype in a day to de-risk?
- Who else needs to approve or review this?

## Wrap
- Read the summary back. Ask: "What did I get wrong? What is missing?"
- Ask for the project's name and the one sentence you would put in the README.
